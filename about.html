    • Our Story

    At V & V, we believe agriculture is more than just farming—it’s the future of entrepreneurship. Unlike traditional educational platforms or agri-traders, we blend learning with earning, creating opportunities for individuals to grow while contributing to the collective success of the community. With a community-first approach, automation, and transparency at the core, we have quickly established ourselves as early movers in a space with no real competition.

    In a short span, V & V has empowered over 5,000 agripreneurs across India, launched pilot programs in agro-waste marketplace transactions, and built meaningful collaborations between Indian learners and international experts, including Russian agricultural specialists. Our efforts have already earned recognition as a promising new-age agri-entrepreneurship initiative, opening doors to sustainable opportunities in the sector.

    Guided by visionary leadership—Mr. Vishnu (Founder), Ms. Shiwangi, Mr. Shivam, and Mr. Atul—and strengthened by an advisory board of world-renowned experts such as Dr. Elena Kravtsov (Agri-Biotech Researcher), Dr. Arjun Dev (Agri-Economist), and Prof. Michael Howard (Sustainable Farming Systems), we ensure our learners gain access to global knowledge and real-world expertise. For us, V & V is not just a platform—it is a revolution in agriculture and entrepreneurship, turning knowledge into wealth, agriculture into opportunity, and individuals into empowered agripreneurs.

    Our Journey

    The story of V & V began when our mentor, Mr. Vishnu, saw the enormous waste of potential in India’s agriculture sector. While nations across the world were innovating with agro by-products and waste, much of India’s wealth in this field went unnoticed or underutilized.

    Founded in 2024, with the guidance of mentors from En+ Group, Russia’s leading aluminium and hydroelectric power company, V & V was created as a necessity — to fill the gap with innovation, learning, and entrepreneurship.

    What started as a vision to regulate agro by-product trade has now grown into a community-driven movement reshaping agriculture into a source of wealth and opportunity.
