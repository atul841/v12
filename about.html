    • Our Story

    Who We Are
    V & V is a pioneering Russian–Indian collaboration designed to empower the next generation of agripreneurs. We bring together Russia’s depth of research and India’s entrepreneurial energy to create a unique ecosystem where agriculture, technology, and entrepreneurship merge.
    Our name itself reflects our roots:

  • “Vladimir” (Russian for great power) stands for strength and innovation.

  • “Vishnu” (Sanskrit for the all-pervasive preserver and protector of the universe) stands for prosperity and continuity.

    • Together, they symbolize the collaboration of two powers that will transform the lives of millions of people.

    Our Journey
    The story of V & V began when our mentor, Mr. Vishnu, saw the enormous waste of potential in India’s agriculture sector. While nations across the world were innovating with agro by-products and waste, much of India’s wealth in this field went unnoticed or underutilized.
    Founded in 2024, with the guidance of mentors from En+ Group, Russia’s leading aluminium and hydroelectric power company, V & V was created as a necessity — to fill the gap with innovation, learning, and
    entrepreneurship.
    What started as a vision to regulate agro by-product trade has now grown into a community-driven movement reshaping agriculture into a source of wealth and opportunity.

    What Makes Us Different
    Unlike traditional educational platforms or agri-traders, V & V combines
    learning with earning. We are:

  • Early movers in a space with no real competitors.

  • Backed by a community-first approach, ensuring collective growth.

  • Powered by automation and transparency, so every member can trust the process.

    • Our Achievements

    In a short span, V & V has:

  • Empowered 5,000+ agripreneurs across India.

  • Launched pilot programs for agro-waste marketplace transactions.

  • Built bridges between Indian learners and Russian experts in agriculture.

  • Earned recognition as a promising new-age agri-entrepreneurship initiative.

    • Our Team

    Our core leadership includes Mr. Vishnu (Founder), along with Ms. Shiwangi , Mr. Shivam and Mr. Atul. Together, they are supported by educators, entrepreneurs, and advisors from diverse industries.
    To further strengthen our foundation, V & V works with an advisory board of scientists, researchers, and PhD scholars from around the world Like Dr. Elena Kravtsov – Agri-Biotech Researcher, Dr. Arjun Dev – Agri-Economist, Prof. Michael Howard – Sustainable Farming Systems . Their knowledge and experience ensure that our learners get access to world-class expertise.
